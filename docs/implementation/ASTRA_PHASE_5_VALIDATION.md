# Student planning — 2026-10-14

Active students can save publicly eligible groups and manage their own bounded shortlist. Archived groups and groups whose teacher becomes unavailable remain as generic unavailable entries; names and teacher details are omitted. Physical group deletion removes its bookmark through the foreign key. Save is idempotent and removal remains possible for unavailable entries. Direct inserts are denied, and active-student ownership applies to table reads/deletes and RPCs.

Before requesting enrollment, students see informational weekly or dated overlap warnings. The database compares accepted, nonarchived groups with the eligible candidate; weekly times use Cairo weekdays, dated intervals use absolute instants, and mixed weekly/dated cases instantiate Cairo occurrences. Two candidate weekly occurrences handle midnight/multiple-day intervals without expanding unbounded date ranges. Touching endpoints, past/completed/cancelled lessons and archived accepted groups do not produce warnings. Only two booleans leave the RPC. Enrollment rules are unchanged.
