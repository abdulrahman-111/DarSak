# Monthly teacher access

First signup/backfill grants one Cairo calendar month independently of initial approval. Free renewal opens seven Cairo calendar days before expiry and is serialized against duplicate grants. Early renewal retains current access; expired renewal restores it immediately. Account/profile/contact/photo/selection/review/inbox remain available while teaching operations pause. Accepted student/admin history and materials remain available. Public discovery/new enrollment exclude expired teachers. Fees are disabled at rollout; no charges or checkout are implemented.

Dedicated admin controls can explicitly confirm a future policy announcement and review later paid-mode requests. Canonical immutable announcements feed private notifications. Disabling fees lets an obsolete pending request be fulfilled by free renewal without a second administrative grant.
