# Adoption and evidence

## Maintainer-reported use

On 23 October 2026, the maintainer reported that teachers and students at their university use DarSak. Participant names are intentionally not published. This is a maintainer report, not an independent audit of usage.

At the time of this record, aggregate user counts, first-use dates, usage frequency, and specific feedback were not yet documented. Do not infer those details from the synthetic demo, CI, screenshots, repository stars, or this statement.

## How to strengthen this record

With participants' permission and without publishing identifying information, record dated aggregate participation, repeat usage, workflows completed, feedback themes, and fixes responding to those themes. State whether evidence is directly observed, participant-reported, or derived from appropriately configured analytics. Clearly label estimates and unknown values.

An independent developer deployment remains a separate evidence item. Automated fresh-clone verification by the maintainer or their coding assistant proves technical reproducibility; it does not establish outside adoption or contribution.

The [pilot and evidence guide](pilot/README.md) provides task cards, feedback questions, and an empty aggregate-results template. Use the existing university community voluntarily rather than inventing activity or collecting names for an application.
